Q: What is different about this library?

A: First, there are no requests for donations. Next, this work is entirely a one man show. Most importantly, this library is a curated selection intended for self learners, independent thinkers, and truth seekers. This means that I favor books that excite the mind to learning and are written with lively interest opposed to deadening and abstract academic works.

Q: Have you read all the books?

A: No. You can assume, however, I've read my favorites.

Q: Where should I start reading?

A: You should be able to find a book at your level. You should always start where you take great interest to maintain motivation. The more you develop your mind, the more directed is your interest and motivation. If you have not read much I can suggest Isaac Asimov's Historia Universal Asimov Series. Another next step is The Story of Civilization by Will Durant. Reading these books give you a foundational layout of human history. This is a geat start to stimulate the thinking mind.

How can I contribute?

I have projects that require certain talents and help. Note: all collaboration work is intended to go into the public domain.

  1. AI Image Generators with No Restrictions. Sometimes AI blocks some Classical Art because of extreme nudity censors. I could use some one off help in these cases to improve the art collections/galleries on this site.

  2. Audiobook Voice Talent. I would love to collaborate with voice talent on different levels of literature. One dream is to produce the poetic works of the iliad and Odyssey written by George Chapman. There are many other fantastic and accessible works to create audiobooks.

  3. Proofreading public domain texts. It's rare to find persons interested in this kind of work because it's the slowest and most tedious work. I have dramatically increased my productivity in transcribing books at high quality, but human proofreading is the last necessary skill needed to finish books.

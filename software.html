To get the most out of your text-first reading experience, I have found the following apps most useful:

Audio: Sirin App for Android. As good as Audible app for audio files library. https://shevchenko.tech/

Ebook: PocketBook app for Android. reads epubs and pdfs with text to speech. https://pocketbook.ch/en-ch/app

Cloud Storage: Pcloud free or paid service. very useful for transferring files (ebooks) from your e-library to all your devices.
